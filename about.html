Meet the Kingdom in the Sky

A LAND OF HIGHLANDS

Discover a place with its own rhythm.

Lesotho is a landlocked country in southern Africa, surrounded by South Africa. Its high elevation gives the country striking mountain scenery, cool highland weather and many opportunities for outdoor travel.

Basotho culture is expressed through language, music, crafts, food, community life and iconic clothing such as the Basotho blanket and mokorotlo hat. Visitors can learn a great deal by listening to local guides and engaging respectfully with communities.

  • Experience highland landscapes and rural villages.
  • Learn about Basotho heritage and traditions.
  • Support local guides, makers and accommodation providers.

TRAVEL VALUES

Explore thoughtfully

01

Respect culture

Ask before photographing people, respect local customs and follow your guide's advice.

02

Protect nature

Stay on suitable trails, take rubbish with you and care for sensitive mountain environments.

03

Support locally

Where possible, choose local guides, locally owned stays and community-made crafts.
